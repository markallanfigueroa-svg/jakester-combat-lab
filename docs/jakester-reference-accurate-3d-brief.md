# Officer Jakester — reference-accurate 3D character brief

## Canonical visual reference
The supplied full-body artwork is the definitive design. The character is an imposing, muscular anthropomorphic pig sheriff, **not** a blocky or cute toy.

- **Silhouette:** wide shoulders, massive defined upper arms and forearms, tapered waist, muscular thighs, sturdy stance and realistic joint proportions.
- **Head:** reddish-pink skin, projecting pig snout and nostrils, large swept pig ears, furrowed brows, intense eyes, short swept/spiky black hair, full black beard, facial scars.
- **Clothing:** rolled-sleeve orange-and-black plaid shirt, layered black/brown tactical vest with pouches and straps, radio, prominent gold sheriff star, wide belt with star buckle, holster, dark cargo trousers, reinforced knee pads, fingerless black gloves and heavy lace-up black boots.
- **Finish:** high-detail stylized realistic game character, distinct fabric/leather/metal surfaces, natural skin and anatomy.

## Build requirements
1. Create a single coherent, anatomically sculpted 3D mesh from front/side/back reference views. Do not claim a 2D image alone supplies hidden geometry accurately.
2. Use a **skinned skeleton** with spine, neck, head, clavicles, shoulders, elbows, wrists, pelvis, hips, knees and ankles; add hand/finger bones as practical.
3. Rig garments and equipment without clipping during large attacks. Preserve original character identity.
4. Export an optimized glTF/GLB for the browser, with textures and skeletal animations. Keep the current procedural v8 workshop available as a fallback until a verified replacement works on iPhone.
5. Validate idle, walk, jab, cross, **right-hand heavy with left-hand guard**, hip-driven roundhouse toward the opponent, crouch, jump, block, dodge, throw and reversal. Full arm extension and smooth recovery required.
6. Free-first Blender workflow. No paid asset services without authorization.

## Status
**Design and acceptance specification only.** No sculpted, textured, skinned 3D character has been built or tested yet. Existing workshop geometry is a procedural prototype.
